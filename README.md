<h1>⚡ Neon Overdrive: Infinite Survivor</h1>

遊戲特色與亮點介紹 | Game Features & Highlights

1. 🎨 視覺與戰鬥視覺效果 | Visuals & Smooth Arcade Combat

同心圓脈衝核心 (Concentric Circle Core)

[中] 玩家角色採用精緻的霓虹多層同心圓設計，具備動態脈衝光輪、方向指標、尾跡粒子與受擊閃光效果。

[EN] The player ship features a stylized multi-layered concentric circle core with dynamic pulsating light rings, direction indicator beads, thruster particle trails, and reactive hit flashes.

流暢的高幀率粒子戰場 (Smooth 60 FPS Particle Canvas)

[中] 支援 60 FPS 流暢畫質，包含滿屏爆炸粒子、擊退與擊中反饋，帶來爽快的割草打擊感。

[EN] High-performance 60 FPS Canvas rendering with rich explosion particles, enemy knockbacks, and tactile hit feedback for an intense survivor experience.

2. ⭐ 無限疊加升級系統 | Infinite Stackable Upgrades

多重戰術無人機 (Stackable Tactical Drones)

[中] 每選擇一次「戰術無人機」，即會多新增一架無人機均勻環繞主角，各自自動鎖定並發射激光。

[EN] Selecting "Tactical Drone" adds an additional drone in orbit around your ship. Drones auto-target and fire laser bolts independently.

無限旋轉光刃 (Stackable Orbiting Plasma Blades)

[中] 光刃數量可無限疊加，形成高速旋轉的死角防護罩，摧毀接近的敵軍。

[EN] Plasma blades stack infinitely, creating a multi-bladed barrier shield that shreds incoming enemies.

多維度屬性強化 (Multi-dimensional Stat Boosts)

[中] 傷害倍率、射速過載、多重彈道、移動速度、子彈體積與水晶磁鐵範圍均可無限次疊加升級。

[EN] Damage multipliers, fire-rate overclocks, multishots, movement speed, projectile sizes, and crystal magnet pull range stack endlessly.

3. 👾 多樣化敵方陣容與 Boss | Varied Enemy Roster & Bosses

豐富敵種分類 (5 Distinct Enemy Types + Sub-entities)

[中] 包含蜂擁而至的追擊者 (Chaser)、極速突襲的疾速者 (Speeder)、擊破後會一分為二的裂變體 (Splitter)、保持距離發射彈幕的遠程狙擊手 (Sniper)，以及高血量的重裝坦克 (Tank)。

[EN] Encounter Chasers (swarming units), Speeders (high-speed harassers), Splitters (splits into twin sub-entities on death), Snipers (long-range artillery), and Tanks (high durability brutes).

無盡霸主 Boss (Dreadnought Boss Spawns)

[中] 每隔 60 秒登場，伴隨全螢幕紅字警示條與專屬巨大血條，考量玩家走位與火力配備。

[EN] Spawned every 60 seconds complete with full-screen warning banners and heavy boss health bars.

4. 💾 雙重存檔與便捷功能 | Dual-Saving & Convenience System

瀏覽器自動臨時存檔 (Automatic Background Auto-Save)

[中] 遊戲每 10 秒及升級時自動儲存進度，斷線或關閉頁面後首頁會顯示「繼續遊戲」按鈕。

[EN] Automatically saves player stats, score, drones, and level progress every 10 seconds. Continue your run directly from the main menu.

一鍵文字存檔碼 (Copy/Paste Encrypted Save Code)

[中] 隨時在暫停或頂部選單複製 Base64 加密存檔碼，可分享給朋友或跨裝置貼上繼續遊玩。

[EN] Instantly copy/paste an encoded Base64 save text string to export or import progress across different machines.

5. 🛠️ 完善的玩家體驗與介面 | Game-Feel & Quality of Life

雙語切換 (Bilingual Toggle zh-Hant / EN)

[中] 隨時可點擊右上角 🌐 按鈕切換繁體中文與英文介面（包含所有卡片說明與敵方檔案）。

[EN] Seamlessly switch all UI strings, upgrade cards, and codex entries between Traditional Chinese and English with one click.

難度選擇系統 (Difficulty System)

[中] 提供 簡單 (Easy)、普通 (Normal) 與 超頻狂暴 (Overdrive) 三種難度，影響敵人數量、血量與得分倍數。

[EN] Choose between Easy, Normal, and Overdrive difficulty settings affecting spawn density, enemy stats, and score multipliers.

玩法與敵方檔案圖鑑 (How to Play & Enemy Codex)

[中] 內建完整操作說明與各敵種特色對照圖鑑，方便玩家隨時查閱。

[EN] Built-in codex modal showcasing controls, mechanics, and detailed threat profiles for all enemy types.

原生音效引擎 (Synthesized Web Audio API)

[中] 純程式碼即時合成發射聲、打擊感、爆炸聲與升級音效，無需加載任何外部音檔。

[EN] Zero-dependency Web Audio synthesizer providing immediate sound effects for laser shots, explosions, level-up chimes, and alerts.
